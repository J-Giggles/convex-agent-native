# Community code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn, ask questions, disagree constructively, and build reliable agent-native software together.

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful, patient, and generous with context;
- critique ideas and code, not people;
- welcome newcomers and questions asked in good faith;
- acknowledge sources and the work that made new work possible;
- protect private, personal, and security-sensitive information; and
- accept maintainer decisions about project scope without harassment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or in public interactions representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/J-Giggles/convex-agent-native/security/advisories/new) with `Conduct report` in the title. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details. The maintainer will review reports in good faith, preserve confidentiality where practical, and may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant based on severity and pattern.

Good-faith disagreement and respectful criticism are not violations. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is itself a violation.

This policy applies to repository activity, Discussions, project events, and other spaces officially operated by this project.
