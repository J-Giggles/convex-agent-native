# Governance

`agent-native-convex` is currently maintainer-led. The maintainer sets release scope, compatibility boundaries, and security policy, while design reasoning and material decisions are recorded publicly in issues, pull requests, compatibility notes, and decision records.

Contributors can influence the roadmap through Discussions, focused proposals, review, documentation, tests, and implementation. There is no requirement to become a regular contributor before proposing a change.

Maintainer status may be offered after a sustained record of technically sound, respectful contributions and reliable review. As the maintainer group grows, this document will be updated with an explicit decision and release process.

The project may decline features that automatically expose arbitrary Convex functions, weaken host-derived authorization, duplicate Convex with another primary database, or broaden compatibility claims beyond tested public contracts.
